## Rules we are following
- Do not clone a page per city. Vizag, Andhra Pradesh and India are covered inside each page
  with real coverage copy. Add a city page only when there is something true and specific to
  say about that city (a real client, a local partner, a local fact).
- Never invent numbers, testimonials or prices. Proof is live work only.
- Keep one H1, one primary CTA (WhatsApp), FAQ answers mirrored in FAQPage schema.
